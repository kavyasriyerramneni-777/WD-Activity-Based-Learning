Food Categories

Choose fresh and nutritious foods every day.

Fruits

Fruits provide essential vitamins, minerals, dietary fiber, and plant antioxidants that support heart health, boost immunity, and aid digestion.

Vegetables

Vegetables provide essential vitamins, minerals, dietary fiber, and antioxidants that fuel the body, support immunity, and protect against chronic diseases.

Whole Grains & Millets

Whole grains and millets provide essential B-complex vitamins, antioxidants, and dietary fiber that support energy production, heart health, blood sugar control, and digestion.

Boiled Eggs

boiled eggs provides high-quality protein, essential vitamins, and nutrients that support muscle repair, brain health, and eye protection.

Dairy

Dairy provides a rich source of essential nutrients that support bone strength, muscle repair, and overall body function.

Healthy Eating Tips

Small habits can support a healthier lifestyle.

Drink water regularly throughout the day.

Include a variety of fruits and vegetables.

Choose whole grains more often.

Include protein foods such as beans, lentils, eggs, dairy or suitable alternatives.

Limit excess salt, added sugar and highly processed foods.

Eat balanced meals and pay attention to hunger and fullness.
