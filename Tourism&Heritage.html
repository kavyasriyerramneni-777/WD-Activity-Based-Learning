Travel Tips

Make your trip comfortable, safe and memorable.

🎟️ Book in Advance

Check official attraction websites for ticket prices, entry times and holiday closures.

🎒 Pack Smart

Carry water, comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate clothing and identification.

🙏 Respect Local Culture

Follow monument rules, dress requirements and photography restrictions.

🌦️ Check the Weather

Review the forecast and plan outdoor sightseeing accordingly.

🍲 Try Local Food

Explore regional cuisine and choose clean, reputable food establishments.

📱 Keep Documents Safe

Save your travel details, emergency contacts and confirmed tickets securely.
